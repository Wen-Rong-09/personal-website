Rebalance, Don't De-Risk: Lessons from 10,000 Simulated Retirements Under Market Regime Uncertainty

18 pages · Aug. 2026 · with Filip Zaleski & Lena Ho, NYU Stern

A Monte Carlo simulation of 30-year retirements driven by a two-state (calm/stressed) Markov regime-switching model, fit to 154 years of US market data (1872–2025), comparing asset allocation, withdrawal, and rebalancing policies on failure risk and sustainable withdrawal rate. The baseline 60/40 portfolio with a 4% withdrawal fails in 6.5% of retirements — closely reproducing the classic “4% rule”—and annual rebalancing beats buy-and-hold (6.5% vs. 7.5%), while an age-based glide path doesn't help (6.9%) because it de-risks after the period of greatest danger has passed.
