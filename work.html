Rebalance, Don't De-Risk: Retirement Portfolio Simulation Under Market Regime Uncertainty

Aug. 2026

A two-state Markov-chain Monte Carlo model calibrated on 154 years (1871–2026) of Shiller and FRED data, simulating 10,000 thirty-year retirement paths across calm and stressed market regimes. Annual rebalancing cut portfolio failure risk from 7.5% to 6.5% and improved worst-case outcomes by 55%, validated against Bengen (1994) and the Trinity Study (1998).
